## [1.0.0]

- First release: a valve radio for falling asleep. A long-wave dial with 15 stations: nine sounds (rain, sea, night train, stove, crickets, harbour, stream, wind, brown noise) and six spoken programmes (Sea Forecast, Roll Call, Word Lottery, a 4-7-8 Metronome, Lighthouse Stores, Countdown). Static, a whistle off tune and long-wave fading between and on them.
- Three rare stations: a storm on about one night in five, For Those Awake between 1:30 and 4:30, and a Moon Station around the full moon.
- A timer that ends with the station drifting off into the static. A touch during it keeps the radio on 15 more minutes.
- QSL cards in the morning with one question, an album of 18 cards, and "your wave", the stations you fall asleep to fastest. Haptic tuning with the eyes closed, call signs, English and Russian.
